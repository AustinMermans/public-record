# Public Record — product contract, v0.0.0

Primary job: identify consequential US public disclosures, understand what they measure or change, anticipate the next official release, and inspect the source.

Visual direction: scholarly working paper. White paper, near-black serif headings, tabular numerals, thin horizontal rules, restrained blue evidence and rust event markers. First screen contains useful results. No decorative imagery or promotional hero.

Core: (1) daily overview with a transparent editorial priority rule; (2) searchable release/deadline calendar with Eastern times and calendar export; (3) macro series explorer with level/change history; (4) disclosure stream across government and selected courts; (5) source registry with availability and limitations; (6) methodology, snapshots, and version history. Forecasts and market references remain distinctly labelled; unavailable consensus is never manufactured.

Highest-value sequence: BLS/BEA calendar, FRED macro histories, Federal Register public inspection and published documents, Federal Reserve statements, SEC current filings, selected court RSS. Treasury auctions, patent research, official forecasts and prediction markets are extension candidates. A source directory entry does not imply a working collector.

Each public record requires a stable ID, publisher, original document/feed URL, event/observation date when supplied, capture timestamp, type, and a clear coverage boundary. Scheduled dates never certify publication. Public inspection never certifies legal effectiveness. Court feed absence never certifies docket inactivity. Current revised history never masquerades as original-release history.

Review gate after core: independent design, content, and portfolio-manager reviews. Resolve material defects and obtain explicit reviewer approval before selecting and implementing the most valuable extension. Release v1.0.0 only after build, data checks, interaction/visual review, and live payload verification.
