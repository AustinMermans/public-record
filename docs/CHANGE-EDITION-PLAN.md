# Next increment: a unified change edition

PM recommendation after bounded v1.5 approval. **Implemented, reviewed and released as v1.6.0.** Hosted refresh and public-payload verification succeeded; see REVIEWS.md for scoped evidence. The original acceptance plan is retained below.

Reader job: “Since the previous successful edition, what changed in the economic picture, companies I follow, funding conditions and forecasts—and where should I look next?”

Use already captured evidence before adding another partially integrated provider. Combine existing macro/funding/financial differences with typed comparisons for company filings and forecasts. Lead with compact Economic data, Companies, Funding and Outlook groups; keep complete disclosure/calendar differences accessible below. Selection must be transparent and bounded, not an invented importance score. Link every item to original evidence and its analytical profile/chart. Group derived recalculations with their input change instead of counting them as independent developments.

Required boundaries:

- Independent previous-success baselines per channel; stale is not “no change,” and a first capture is only a baseline.
- Newly captured is not newly published. Filing, reporting, forecast publication and collection dates remain distinct.
- New forecast targets/horizons and changed definitions establish boundaries, not numeric revisions. Amendments remain identifiable.
- Rolling-feed disappearance is not withdrawal; schedule rescheduling needs stable identity rather than a date-derived ID.
- Filters for company/domain/change type; summary reconciles to the full ledger with displayed bounds.

Acceptance: a controlled fixture puts one new company accession, revised financial fact, macro revision and same-target GDPNow revision into one useful edition with correct evidence. Cover baseline, stale/resumed, unchanged/metadata-only, definition/target changes and original-versus-comparative facts. Verify a real hosted refresh without seeded demonstration changes. Require fresh PM and temporal-methodology review plus responsive UI checks.

No narrative-generation engine, alerts, private portfolio storage or new provider is included in this increment. Treasury fiscal flows/financing, FDIC banking aggregates, broader forecasts and final whole-product review remain open immediately afterward. The persistent goal is not complete.
