# One-quarter GDP watch

The Outlook watch joins three distinct public information sets by **real GDP growth target quarter**, not by screen position or retrieval day. The [Atlanta Fed GDPNow](https://www.atlantafed.org/research-and-data/data/gdpnow) is a model nowcast of BEA's advance real-GDP growth estimate; it is not an official Federal Reserve forecast. The [Philadelphia Fed SPF](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/surveys-and-data/real-time-data-research/median-forecasts) RGDP value is annualized quarterly growth derived from median forecast **levels**, not the median of individual growth-rate forecasts. [BEA's dated GDP release](https://www.bea.gov/news/schedule/full) is an observation only after the advance news page is verified. FOMC projections use a different annual GDP horizon and never enter this quarterly comparison.

All three rates refer to quarter-over-quarter real GDP growth at an annualized rate, but their methods and information dates differ. The current capture's 2026 Q3 watch shows GDPNow 5.0% as of September 25 and SPF 2.4624% from the August 14 survey (displayed as 2.46%). The descriptive contrast is about 2.5 percentage points. This is **not** a contemporaneous consensus spread, a market-implied expectation, a realized surprise, or evidence that one forecaster is superior. BEA's 2026 Q3 advance remains scheduled for October 29 in this capture; a calendar date does not supply an actual value. Every number retains a direct publisher link, publication date and separate capture clock.

The compact numerical contrast appears only when both same-target forecasts are verified, non-stale and recently published relative to the reading date: GDPNow within 14 days and SPF within 100 days. It also requires a healthy BEA schedule with that target's advance date **still in the future** and no verified advance release. On the scheduled release date, the prominent contrast is withheld until the official publication state is checked, because date-only data cannot establish that it has not yet occurred. A historical or missing row remains visible with its date or explicit absence but does not become a zero or a current comparison. BEA's later second/third stages, today's revised FRED history, and the FOMC's annual projections cannot fill the advance slot. An advance value can link to the full GDP stage record without becoming a forecast-error claim.

The SPF historical points are cells in the **currently retrieved** official workbook. The source can revise old cells; these are not archived original-release files. A defensible real-time error history would require a final pre-advance GDPNow value and appropriately dated original SPF evidence paired with the BEA advance estimate for the same target, with publication order verified. The current GDPNow collector retains only the latest page estimate, so that backtest is not produced. On September 29, 2026, the Atlanta Fed's published historical-workbook link returned a 404 page to this collector; this access observation is not evidence that the history does not exist. No proprietary Blue Chip values are ingested.
