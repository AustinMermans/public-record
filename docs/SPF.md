# Survey of Professional Forecasters

Public Record reads the Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia's [median forecast workbooks](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/surveys-and-data/real-time-data-research/median-forecasts) and [release-date file](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/-/media/FRBP/Assets/Surveys-And-Data/survey-of-professional-forecasters/spf-release-dates.txt). The source's [documentation](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/-/media/FRBP/Assets/Surveys-And-Data/survey-of-professional-forecasters/spf-documentation.pdf) controls definitions. These are survey medians, not Federal Reserve policy commitments or GDPNow model estimates.

The six extracted measures are RGDP, UNEMP, CPI, CORECPI, PCE and COREPCE. RGDP is annualized quarter-over-quarter growth of median forecast levels, not the median of individual growth forecasts. The inflation measures are medians of directly forecast annualized quarterly rates. UNEMP is the forecast quarterly average unemployment rate. All displayed numbers are percent; differences between survey forecasts are percentage points. The quarter in a selector is the **forecast target**, not the survey quarter: suffix 2 in the source is the current survey quarter and suffixes 3–6 are the next four quarters. A revision compares the same measure and calendar target across successive survey releases, even though its source column changes. A target appearing for the first time has no numerical revision.

The source says release dates before 1990 Q2 are not known, and its 1990 Q2 date is retrospective. Public Record starts the date-attributed history in 1990 Q3 and excludes an observation until the day *after* its documented release date, because a date-only record cannot prove release time. A successful retrieval retains the complete source files by SHA-256. The build re-parses those files and rejects values that do not reconcile to the retained cells. An outage retains the last success with an explicit stale status; an initial outage publishes no forecast.

Historical workbook cells are from the **currently published workbook edition**. They can be corrected by the publisher; they are not archived copies of files as available to a forecaster on each old release date. A changed historical cell is recorded separately from a newly issued survey forecast. The collector excludes proprietary historical Moody's Aaa/Baa values, recession-probability interpretations, confidence intervals inferred from medians and unverified future release events. Source data remains subject to its publisher's terms and is not relicensed by Public Record.
